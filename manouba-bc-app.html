What We Offer

Command Every Corner of Your Business

Six powerful modules, one golden dashboard. Everything your SME needs to operate, analyze, and grow.

👥
HR Management
Employee profiles, attendance, payroll, leave management, and performance reviews — all under one roof.
📦
Inventory Control
Real-time stock tracking, low-stock alerts, supplier management, purchase orders, and barcode scanning.
📊
Analytics Dashboard
Interactive charts for revenue, expenses, inventory turnover, and KPIs — filterable by date and department.
📄
PDF Report Engine
Automated branded reports generated server-side in under 2 seconds. Scheduled delivery to your inbox.
🔐
Role-Based Access
Admin, Manager, and Employee roles with granular permission control. JWT auth with refresh token rotation.
🏢
Multi-Tenant SaaS
Multiple businesses on one deployment. Complete data isolation, custom branding, independent settings per tenant.
